Campus Energy Audit and Action Plan:

This dashboard provides a comprehensive visual overview of institutional energy patterns to support sustainability goals and behavioral change among campus occupants. 

Dashboard Overview:

The dashboard is designed to monitor current demand and identifying consumption trends across different sectors of the campus. 
Energy Consumption by Departments: A line chart tracking historical use across various academic or administrative units to benchmark performance.
Avg. Energy Consumption by Weekday: A bar chart showing how usage fluctuates throughout the week. For example, Monday is identified as the day with minimum consumption.
Renewable Energy by Departments: Tracks power produced by clean sources, such as rooftop solar panels.
Energy Consumption by Area: A granular view of energy use intensity (EUI), often visualized as a campus map or detailed trend line to identify specific buildings with high demand. 

Key Insights & Action Plan:

The dashboard facilitates data-driven decision-making by turning complex utility data into actionable insights. 
Behavioral Change: By visualizing real-time impact, occupants can see the immediate results of conservation efforts, such as turning off lights or adjusting thermostats.
Infrastructure Optimization: Facilities managers use these insights to schedule HVAC corrections for nights, weekends, and academic breaks to reduce waste.
Measurable Goals: The data supports strategic planning by helping to set and track measurable targets, such as carbon footprint reduction goals.
